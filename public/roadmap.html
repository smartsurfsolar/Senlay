Roadmap

A direction and validation sequence for physical-world intelligence for autonomous systems. This is not a delivery schedule.

BETACoordinate-level environmental context
  • Available environmental observations and model values, source provenance, freshness, conflicts and unknowns where supplied.
  • Physical World Model, structured REST responses, risk events, public MCP gateway and signed station ingestion.
  • Read-only robotics reference adapters and synthetic offline replay; real-hardware validation is pending.
EVALUATEValidate evidence and integration workflows
  • Evaluate evidence quality, forecast/observation comparisons, source reliability and geographic representativeness.
  • Explore aerial, marine and field robotics evaluations with prospective users; define data rights, locations and measures before a pilot.
  • Assess integration effort, reliability and usefulness on real operational datasets when agreed and available.
PLANNEDMission environmental verification
  • Explore route and time-window context, platform-specific requirements and changes during an operation.
  • Develop more complete evidence objects and reports and additional adapters where customer needs justify them.
  • Keep planned mission capabilities distinct from current coordinate-level context.
LONG-TERM DIRECTIONInfrastructure for autonomous fleets
  • Expand distributed observations, machine-readable physical evidence and integration with robotics ecosystems.
  • Support environment-aware planning in customer systems without taking over navigation or operational authority.

Current boundary

For drones, Senlay presents wind observations and available model indications of rain and thunderstorms. Local rainfall and lightning are not directly measured by this assessment. Aircraft limits, route, altitude, visibility and operational permission remain outside its scope. Mission routes, platform limits and operational authorization are not evaluated. Data coverage and source rights vary.
