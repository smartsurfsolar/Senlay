The physical-world model

External conditions deserve the same attention as onboard perception.

Weather, water and terrain can change the context around an operation. External information can also be delayed, geographically distant, conflicting or missing. Senlay's Physical World Model brings available observations and models into a structured representation for a location, with their supporting evidence and limits.

Physical data sources→Senlay intelligence & verification→Structured context→Autonomous software & operators
Observe · Verify · Integrate

Make environmental evidence useful to software.

Observe

Request available conditions from environmental networks, connected sensors and forecast models for a coordinate.

Verify

Inspect provenance, timestamps, freshness, distance, disagreement and missing evidence where provided. Comparisons do not establish ground truth.

Integrate

Use structured REST responses, MCP tools and read-only reference adapters alongside your existing application and safety systems.

For developers and agents

Use source-aware context in your stack.

The coordinate API and MCP gateway expose available physical context. Wind evidence for a drone is one concrete workflow; the full collection profile can expose additional available layers. Reference adapters illustrate read-only integration.

Coordinate-level REST request · API key in the Authorization header

curl --get 'https://senlay.cloud/api/v1/pwm' \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $SENLAY_API_KEY" \
  --data-urlencode 'lat=37.775' \
  --data-urlencode 'lng=-122.419' \
  --data-urlencode 'field=general' \
  --data-urlencode 'profile=full'

The response contains available context and evidence; source coverage and freshness vary.

Current capability & direction

From coordinates toward mission-aware intelligence.

Available in the beta: source-aware context at a selected coordinate, subject to coverage and source availability. Planned: interpretation across routes, operating windows and platform constraints. Senlay does not currently provide whole-mission clearance or control a robot.

Origin · SmartSurf

From understanding conditions on the water to a broader physical-world layer.

SmartSurf began with marine conditions, rider and equipment safety, GPS and tracker concepts. That work led to Senlay's approach to connecting location and environmental evidence. It is the product's origin and reference application; robotics demand and field performance remain to be validated.
