Senlay · company brief

Physical-world intelligence for autonomous systems.

Senlay connects environmental observations, forecast models and sensors into a source-aware intelligence layer for robots, autonomous applications and AI agents.

The evaluation beta returns context for a coordinate, making source provenance, freshness, disagreement and missing evidence visible where available. A drone wind workflow demonstrates one application of the broader platform.

Problem and approach

External physical context is fragmented and uncertain.

Robotics software has navigation and onboard sensing, yet changing weather, water and terrain still affect operations. External values can be missing, delayed or unrepresentative. Senlay organizes available evidence so an application can inspect what supports it.

Market focus

Aerial, marine and field autonomy.

Robotics developers, manufacturers and operators are the primary market hypothesis. Wind evidence for drones is one existing evaluation workflow, alongside potential marine and field applications.

Aerial

Wind and atmospheric evidence for drone software and operator review.

Marine

Available waves, tides and coastal observations for surface robotics and maritime applications.

Field

Environmental context for agriculture, outdoor inspection and remote infrastructure.

Product and direction

Evidence at a coordinate today; mission context is planned.

The beta provides available source-aware context, domain risk events, signed station ingestion, MCP and reference adapters. Route/time-window evaluation and platform-specific mission support are future work. Coverage, source rights and field performance require validation.

Commercial model hypotheses

Developer API access, scoped pilots, OEM integration and later fleet or private deployment offerings. Paid subscriptions are planned and unlaunched.

Validation priorities

Test evidence usefulness, integration, reliability and willingness to pay with prospective users. No external robotics pilot results or customer outcomes are claimed here.

Founder and origin

From SmartSurf and marine conditions to physical-world infrastructure.

SmartSurf began with rider and equipment safety, location and tracker concepts. Connecting those needs to environmental evidence led to Senlay's wider Physical World Model and autonomous-systems direction.

What makes the approach distinct

The proposed combination is source-aware environmental context, provenance, freshness, uncertainty, source comparison and flexible interfaces. Accuracy and competitive advantage require independent evidence.
