01 · Identity

Physical-world intelligence for autonomous systems.

02 · Problem

External conditions are fragmented and uncertain.

Weather, water and terrain affect physical operations. Data may be stale, distant, inconsistent or missing; software needs the evidence behind a value.

Senlay · evaluation beta02 / 09

03 · Product

A Physical World Model for available environmental evidence.

Senlay assembles context at a coordinate and exposes provenance, freshness, source disagreement and unknowns where available.

Senlay · evaluation beta03 / 09

04 · Technology

Observe, verify and integrate.

Observations and models → Senlay intelligence and verification → structured REST/MCP context → autonomous software and operators. Comparison does not establish ground truth.

Senlay · evaluation beta04 / 09

05 · Market focus

Aerial, marine and field autonomy.

Drone software, unmanned surface vessels, outdoor inspection and agricultural robotics are market hypotheses. A drone wind-evidence workflow is one implemented example.

Senlay · evaluation beta05 / 09

06 · Current capability

Coordinate-level context in an evaluation beta.

The API, MCP gateway, risk events, signed station ingestion and read-only reference adapters exist. Coverage, data rights and field performance depend on source and location.

Senlay · evaluation beta06 / 09

07 · Direction

Mission environmental verification is planned.

Future work may include route and time-window context, platform-specific requirements and evidence reports. The current product does not authorize missions or control robots.

Senlay · evaluation beta07 / 09

08 · Origin and validation

SmartSurf led from marine safety questions to a broader platform.

Tracker and location concepts and environmental context informed Senlay. Robotics customer demand, field performance and willingness to pay remain to be validated.

Senlay · evaluation beta08 / 09

09 · Business hypothesis

Build the case with scoped evaluations.

Potential models include API access, enterprise pilots, OEM integrations and future fleet or private deployment offerings. Paid subscriptions are planned and unlaunched; commercial source rights must be confirmed.
